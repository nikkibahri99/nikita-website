Forever New had a backlog of unsold prior-season inventory alongside an upcoming new season launch. Brand Strategy
Sequenced a relaunch the right way round
A premium UAE furniture brand was relaunching after a year offline. Designed a two-phase plan: six months of brand awareness before pushing sales. Sequenced it that way deliberately because pushing conversion too early on a relaunching premium brand rarely works.
Approach: Awareness first, conversion second
